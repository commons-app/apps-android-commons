# Files

- [Account and authentication flow](account-and-authentication.md) - Traces interactive MediaWiki login, two-factor and email challenges, Android account and preference ownership, cookie and CSRF recovery, logout, and anonymous navigation. Also records beta configuration boundaries and the tests that protect the flow.
- [Discovery, search, and contribution browsing](discovery-and-contributions.md) - Explains how Commons media, category, depiction, and contribution results move from search and navigation through paged remote sources and local caches. Covers continuation state, recent searches, empty and error handling, media details, bookmarks, and hand-off to editing or upload flows.
- [Media selection, preparation, and upload lifecycle](media-upload.md) - Traces external share, picker, camera, and in-app contribution inputs through metadata and license preparation, durable Room queueing, foreground WorkManager execution, resumable MediaWiki stash upload, Wikidata edits, notifications, and recovery.
- [Nearby places and location](nearby-and-location.md) - Explains how Nearby obtains and classifies device location, chooses radial or map-bounds Wikidata searches, enriches results from cache and network, and presents place markers. Also documents how the location picker returns coordinates to uploads or edits existing media metadata.
- [Profile, settings, bookmarks, notifications, campaigns, and quiz](profile-settings-and-notifications.md) - How Commons presents account profiles, persists preferences and bookmarks, retrieves Wikimedia notifications and campaigns, and decides when to prompt the revert-rate quiz. The page separates account-scoped, preference-scoped, Room or content-provider-backed, and remote state, including logged-out and failure behavior.
- [Review, deletion, page edits, and image editing](review-delete-and-edit.md) - Traces local review persistence, review actions, authenticated MediaWiki edits and deletion nominations, reason construction, thanks, captions and descriptions, and the Jpegtran-based image editor including failure and cancellation boundaries.
